## Combat and loot decisions

Published combat stats, melee style, attack interval, maximum hit and directional
melee defence use the existing source-combat architecture. One source-owned native
loot outcome is selected once per death, then TrinityCore supplies guaranteed
items and uniform quantity ranges. No alternate combat or persistence system is
introduced. Members-style ordinary main tables apply to every Warscape player;
F2P replacements are excluded rather than stacked with seeds/herbs. Shared gems
retain no Ring of Wealth, surface Nature talisman and pre-Legends quest policy.
All items are still stackable collectibles without functional equipment, currency,
food, crafting, farming or sceptre-assembly behaviour. Noted drops use the same
collectible item; no note exchange is implemented.

- Warriors include 17 melee Barbarians, six town Warrior source appearances and
  one Al Kharid warrior. Barbarian source Aitan (ID 3068) is ranged and excluded.
  Low/high Barbarian main tables differ. Town Warriors drop only Bones; their
  pickpocket rewards are not kill loot. Al Kharid uses the shared human members
  table, not pickpocket Coins. Native WoW aggro replaces the unimplemented special
  Al Kharid ally-assistance rule.
- Knights include hostile/passive male/female Black Knights and four White
  Knight tiers with male/female variants. Their individual main tables, ranges,
  source stats and native hostility are retained. Black Knight one-Coin chance
  remains 14/128; the Wanted! quest's guaranteed-Coin override is not applied.
- The general seed-table source module puts integer combat levels 1-48 entirely
  in its first tier (threshold 485 after level times ten). Its reviewed counts
  are 368/276/184/92/46/23/11/8 out of 1008 for Potato/Onion/Cabbage/Tomato/
  Sweetcorn/Strawberry/Watermelon/Snape grass seeds, with source quantities.
  `general_seed_table` explicitly rejects higher tiers pending a reviewed
  extension; this batch does not substitute the first tier for stronger enemies.
- Uncommon seeds retain each listed source weight over 1048. Both the Wiki table
  and template list weights totalling 1046/1048; the unlisted 2/1048 is explicitly
  empty. This is an authoring assumption, not a source-confirmed Nothing drop.
  Listed seed chances are not renormalized. The gap only affects this batch's
  uncommon-seed branch; it does not alter earlier releases.
- Hobgoblins use distinct armed/unarmed main tables and source stats. Members
  coin, Goblin mail and Nothing substitutions are applied exactly; Karamja's
  alternate Trading sticks are not stacked with Coins. These prototypes use
  Coins everywhere in the custom world. GWD Bandos-item protection is deferred.
  Missing Hobgoblin respawn fields use regular 35 ticks as metadata.
- Flesh Crawlers use the members 100-slot table, a 35/100 herb branch and a source
  maximum hit of one despite higher-tier stats. They have no guaranteed Bones
  or Ashes: Ashes is a 4/100 outcome and Bottom of sceptre is 3/100. Sceptre use
  and the distinct F2P table remain unimplemented policies.
- Ogres always drop Big bones and roll 19/128 on uncommon seeds; the other
  109/128 adds no item. GWD faction protection is deferred and all three use
  native hostile WoW AI. The uncommon table gap still leaves Big bones intact.
- Wilderness Bandit 22 uses its full members main table. Bandit 130 is excluded
  because its XP modifier needs separate combat support. Pollnivneach Bandits
  41/56 drop Bones only; blackjacking/pickpocketing is not implemented. Rogue 15
  uses the human members table. Rogue 135 is deferred, including its large
  guaranteed coin range and chest/Thieving interaction.
- Missing Slayer-XP fields for non-Slayer human variants use HP only as reference
  metadata. This does not grant Slayer XP or create tasks. All selected variants
  have zero source XP bonus, so no published XP bonus is silently dropped.
- Clues, Wilderness/Slayer tertiary loot, quest overrides, ensouled heads,
  pickpocketing, blackjacking, equipment/faction conditions, magical/ranged
  attacks and elemental weaknesses remain separate milestones.

## Models and equipment

model direction; male/female source variants use matching human displays. White
Knights use Stormwind male/female plate guards. Black Knights use a Dalaran male
plate guard and Scarlet female plate guard: armour colours are approximate and
are not a custom black-armour reskin. User visual acceptance is required.
Hobgoblins use scaled rough-clothed Peon orcs with spears on armed forms. Ogres
use classic Boulderfist displays; Flesh Crawlers use the Silithid Grub model.
Bandits/Rogue use Defias leather/cutpurse displays and swords/daggers.

46 new equipment rows add native weapons/shields through existing
`creature_equip_template` and summon equipment loading. Equipment is cosmetic;
source profile combat remains authoritative. The model audit validates every
hand item against Item.dbc inventory types. Every newly selected display has
stand/walk/run/attack/death sequences. This does not prove sound, proportions,
weapon attachment, armour colours or combat feel in-game.

60 new collectible item entries are 902115-902174. Existing names/IDs for Bones,
Coins, herbs, ores and other prior loot are reused. Client/server Item.dbc append
only these records and preserve all earlier rows/strings and unrelated MPQ
payloads. There are no client executable, object lifetime, field-map or memory
layout changes. The verified deployment targets remain `C:\Warscape\Server`
and `C:\Wrath of the Lich King` as in prior accepted checkpoints.
